What we are looking for:
We are looking for a highly motivated individual to work closely with our research scientists on deep research and rapid prototyping with Large Language Models, with a focus on multilingual language understanding, translation, reasoning, and domain adaptation. The intern will investigate how modern LLMs can be adapted, analyzed, and improved for multilingual and entertainment-domain applications, while developing research ideas that can potentially translate into publications, prototypes, and production impact. The role is research-intensive: we are looking for someone who is interested in understanding why models behave the way they do and developing new methods, rather than primarily integrating existing models or running standard fine-tuning pipelines.

What you'll do:
Conduct research on LLMs for multilingual language understanding and generation, cross-lingual transfer, language interference, model behaviour, and multilingual reasoning particularly across Indian and international languages.
Explore domain adaptation for entertainment content, including subtitles, dialogues, scripts, and conversational language.
Explore reasoning and adaptive computation for translation, including when and how additional reasoning or context should be used.
Where relevant, explore reinforcement learning, preference optimization, bandits, or other learning-based approaches for improving LLM/NMT behavior.
Rapidly prototype research ideas using modern LLM/NMT frameworks and evaluate them on both research benchmarks and real-world entertainment data.
Collaborate closely with research scientists to formulate hypotheses, implement experiments, analyze results, and contribute to research papers, patents, and internal prototypes.
Fine-tuning LLMs (LoRA, QLoRA, Instruction Tuning)
